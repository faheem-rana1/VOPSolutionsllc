Finding a starting point

Transformation questions

Do we need to replace every system?

That should not be the starting assumption. First identify the process and outcome, then consider which existing systems support them and where modernization may be useful.

How is this different from buying new software?

The scope includes business processes, workflow redesign, a technology roadmap and adoption planning. Software is one possible part of a broader change in how work happens.

Can we begin with a single inefficient process?

Yes. A specific example makes the desired outcome easier to discuss. Explain who performs the work, which systems are involved and where employees or customers encounter difficulty before deciding how broad the technology changes need to be.
