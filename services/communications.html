Before you choose

Collaboration questions

Can we discuss tools we already use?

Yes. Your current environment is the starting point. Bring a list of calling, meeting and file-sharing tools, along with the problems employees encounter.

Is Microsoft Teams the only option?

Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365 are supported areas, alongside cloud telephony, VoIP and other collaboration needs. Recommendations depend on your requirements rather than a single default platform.

What matters for a team split between home and office?

Describe how people join meetings, receive calls and access shared files in each location. That gives the platform discussion a practical basis and helps distinguish communication needs from the separate devices and connections that support them.
