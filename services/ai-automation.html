Before you begin

Automation questions

Does every automation need AI?

No. A repeatable, rules-based task may be better suited to workflow automation. We consider the process before choosing an AI-assisted approach.

What should we bring to a first conversation?

Describe one repetitive task, the systems involved, the information used and where human review matters. Avoid sharing sensitive business data in an initial inquiry.

How do data access and accuracy affect the choice?

Identify which information the process needs, who is allowed to use it and how results will be checked. Those requirements belong alongside integration and cost when deciding whether an AI assistant or a simpler automation is appropriate.
