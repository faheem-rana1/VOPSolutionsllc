Defining the arrangement

Managed IT questions

Can you work alongside our internal IT team?

Yes. Support can supplement an existing team. Discuss which responsibilities stay with your staff and where additional assistance would be useful before defining the arrangement.

What support coverage is included?

Coverage depends on your needs and the agreed scope. Discuss availability, responsibilities and response expectations with us so you know what your support arrangement includes.

What helps define the right level of assistance?

Describe your users, devices, important applications and current support responsibilities. Include recurring issues, vendor relationships and any backup or recovery planning needs. This makes it easier to discuss where help is needed without assuming that every organization requires the same arrangement.
