About this notice

This notice describes the VOP Solutions LLC informational website. Client services and the handling of client systems or data are addressed separately in the agreements for those services.

Contacting us

The contact page provides email and telephone links rather than an online submission form. An email link opens your email application; it does not send a message automatically. If you choose to contact us, your message may include your name, contact details, business information and the information you decide to share.

Inquiry information is intended to help us respond and discuss requested services. Please do not send passwords, payment details or confidential client information in an initial inquiry.

Website requests and external services

Opening a website involves sending technical information, such as your IP address and browser information, to the hosting service. This website also requests fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests are handled by the respective providers.

For information about font requests, see Google Fonts privacy information. Email and telephone communications are also handled by the providers you and VOP Solutions use.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, account sign-in or code that sets cookies. This describes the website code, not every practice of external service providers.

Privacy questions

Changes to this notice

The notice should be updated when the website or its information-handling practices change. Adding a contact form, analytics or other integrations requires a fresh review.
