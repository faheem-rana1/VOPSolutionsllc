Setting expectations

Security questions

Is antivirus enough on its own?

It is only one part of the picture. Our service scope also covers identities, email, networks, cloud applications, data protection and security awareness.

Does this guarantee that an incident cannot happen?

No. Security work addresses risk; it does not remove every risk. The appropriate controls and scope depend on your environment and business requirements.

Where should we start if our priorities are unclear?

Begin by describing the users, devices, applications and information your business depends on. A security assessment can help frame the gaps to discuss. Include current backup and access arrangements, but do not send credentials or confidential security details through an initial inquiry.

Clarify your next security step.

Describe your environment and concerns without including passwords or sensitive account information.
