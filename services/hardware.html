Before purchasing

Procurement questions

Can you help beyond choosing a device?

Yes. The service covers evaluation, sourcing, configuration and integration. The specific equipment and work involved should be discussed in relation to your current environment.

What information helps with equipment selection?

Bring the intended use, current equipment, required applications, quantities and budget. Include any compatibility concerns so the discussion is based on what the equipment must actually do.

Should we discuss existing equipment before buying replacements?

Yes. Knowing what is already in use helps explain the requirements for new technology. Describe which devices need replacing, what must remain compatible and whether the purchase supports an immediate need or a longer-term change in your environment.
